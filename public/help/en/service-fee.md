# How Are bbdbuy Service Fees Charged?

## 🛒 bbdbuy Purchasing Service Description

- **✅ Free purchasing service for general platforms**
  bbdbuy does not charge any purchasing fee for orders from general shopping platforms (only product price + domestic shipping apply).

- **💼 Special platform service fee**
  A 10 RMB fee per item applies for special platforms such as Xianyu, Paipai, Zhuanzhuan, Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Youzan, etc.

## 💰 How Total Purchasing Cost is Calculated

> **Total Cost = Product Cost + International Shipping Fee**

- **📦 Product cost**
  Product price + domestic shipping (seller → bbdbuy warehouse)

- **✈️ International shipping includes**
  Basic freight, customs fee (10–30 RMB), warehouse handling fee (8 RMB), optional value-added services
