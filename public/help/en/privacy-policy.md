## 1. What information do we collect from you?

Generally, you can visit our company on the Internet without telling us who you are or providing any personal information about yourself. However, for your convenience in enjoying our services, you may choose to provide us with the following relevant information in various circumstances.

### 1) Information you provide to us
When you register for a bbdbuy account, we will save the basic information you provide: email address and password. If you wish, you can provide more information in your profile when you use our services, including: name, gender, photo, birthday, contact number, country/region, education level, and work experience.

### 2) Actions you perform
When you use our services, we automatically collect some data to enable these services to better meet your needs. Such data includes:
*   Content you search for
*   Products/shops you visit
*   Date, time stamps, and your communications with customer service
*   Videos you watch
*   Ads you click or tap on
*   Your location information
*   Device information
*   IP address and Cookie data

### 1) Contacting you regarding your account
*   To conclude a contract with you and provide you with our services, comply with our legal obligations, protect your vital interests, or use as needed for public interest, to broaden the scope of our services to you.
*   To provide payment processing and account management, operate, measure, and improve our services, and maintain the security, stability, and operability of our services.
*   To contact you via email, telephone, SMS/text message, mail, and mobile device push notifications to resolve account issues, handle disputes, collect fees or debts, or take other necessary actions to provide you with customer service.
*   To detect, prevent, mitigate, and investigate fraud, security vulnerabilities, or potential prohibited or illegal activities.
*   To enforce our membership agreement, this privacy notice, or other policies, and to provide other services in the manner described when we collected the data as requested by you.

### 2) Pursuing our legitimate interests while maintaining your rights and freedoms
We have implemented measures to balance our interests with your rights, including:
*   Improving our services, such as reviewing data related to pages where members encounter stalls or conflicts, so that we can identify and resolve problems and provide you with a better experience.
*   Customizing, evaluating, and improving on-site advertising based on your ad customization preferences.
*   Providing targeted marketing, service updates, and promotional offers based on your communication preferences.
*   Contacting you via email or mail as authorized by applicable law to provide you with coupons, discounts, and special promotions; collecting your opinions through surveys or questionnaires, and notifying you about our services.
*   Inviting you to become our partner and participate in our company's projects via email, telephone, SMS/text message, mail, and mobile device push notifications.
*   Contacting you regarding public policy matters or other current events related to your ability to use our services, which may include inviting you to participate in petitions, letter writing, calling, or other types of public policy-related activities.
*   Accepting valuable opinions or suggestions you give us.
*   Measuring the traffic and click-through rates of various pages on our website and mobile applications, or other promotional effectiveness, through GA/Baidu API.
*   Measuring seller performance (e.g., using shipping tracking information issued by sellers and carriers or provided via bbdbuy).
*   Monitoring and improving the data security of our website and mobile applications.

### 3) With your consent, we may use your personal data for the following purposes
*   Providing you with marketing promotions via telephone, mail, SMS, or text message, including offering you coupons, discounts, and special sales, providing opportunities to use service items on our platform, website content of items and services you may like, tracking your shopping cart, your tracking list, your collections, and collections and sellers you choose to follow, etc.

We will not use automated decision-making technologies to make decisions that have a significant impact on you unless the decision is a necessary part of the contract between us and you, we have obtained your consent, or the law requires us to use such technologies.
*   Please note that unless we have obtained your consent, we will not share your personal data with any advertisers. We will not solicit personal information from children or other persons without the capacity to be responsible for their own conduct without the permission, knowledge, and presence of a guardian, nor will we send them requests for personal information.

### 1) bbdbuy Related Group Companies
May use this data to provide joint content and services, assist in detecting fraud, provide personalized advertising, improve products, etc.

### 2) Service Providers and Financial Institution Partners
Includes third parties assisting us in providing services, payment processing, logistics companies (such as DHL, SAL, FBA, etc.).

### 3) Law Enforcement Agencies, Public Security Authorities, or Other Judicial Bodies and Organizations

If we are merged or acquired by another company, we may share data with them in accordance with our privacy policy.

Your personal information will be accessible from our servers and will be processed on our servers. Regardless of where personal information is stored, we are committed to protecting the privacy and integrity of your personal information. Your personal information will be subject to our technical and organizational controls and governed by our policies and procedures (including this Privacy Policy).

Our company guarantees the security and quality of the information data you provide to us in physical, electronic, etc., forms.

## 5. How long will we retain your information?

Once you successfully register a bbdbuy account, we will permanently save your personal information. If you request, we will also delete your information in advance. We will retain the required data according to our legal obligations.

## 6. How can you exercise your rights regarding your information?

Regarding the personal information we hold about you, you have the right to access, modify, or delete your data, or restrict or refuse to let us process your data:

1.  **Access and Correction**: You can log in to your account to view, review, and update most of your personal data.
2.  **Inquiry**: To request whether an inquiry involves a fee, as permitted by applicable national laws, please contact us.
3.  **Restriction and Objection**: In certain circumstances, apart from storage purposes, you have the right to request that we stop processing the relevant personal information we hold about you.
4.  **Erasure**: Upon your request, we will close your account and remove your personal data viewable by others as soon as reasonably possible.
