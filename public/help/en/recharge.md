# What recharge methods does bbdbuy support?

Log in to your account, click [Personal Center] – [Recharge]

## Recharge and International Credit Card Recharge

- Credit card recharge supports a variety of international cards worldwide (VISA, Master, Discover) and credit cards from some countries.
- Users worldwide can use credit card recharge, facilitating cross-border user transactions and consumption.
- Using credit card payment is safe and reliable. The platform uses advanced encryption technology to ensure the security of payment information and funds.
- Under normal circumstances, the account is received in real time; in special circumstances, it takes 1–5 working days to arrive.

## Local Payment
