# Shipping Restrictions

## 1. When submitting a parcel for delivery, why are some routes unavailable in the delivery method?

In international logistics transportation, there are strict restrictions on relevant item types. When the status of your ordered goods reaches [Warehouse], if there are goods with mailing restrictions or some areas cannot be reached, we will explain with [Reason for Disabling].

*Reminder: After understanding the product characteristics and shipping restrictions, you can contact online customer service to verify whether the mailing restriction can be lifted, but you need to bear the customs risk yourself after the restriction is lifted.*

## 2. Why can't the goods already in the warehouse be submitted for delivery together?

If some goods in the warehouse involve mailing restrictions, they will also be restricted when submitting the parcel waybill for all goods.

## 3. Small-capacity liquids, medicines, etc. should be mixed with other goods. How many pieces are limited?

It is recommended that such goods should not account for more than 10% of all goods in the parcel.

## 4. Why do some items need to be sent separately?

For reasonable packaging or to reduce the volume and weight of the parcel, if the length of a single side is greater than or equal to 70CM, it needs to be packed separately. For details of size restrictions on various logistics routes, please click the link to view.

## 5. What are the benefits of centralized parcel delivery?

If multiple purchased goods are combined for mailing, we provide professional packaging support, which can save international shipping costs compared to separate mailing.

Taking EMS Express Delivery to Singapore as an example:

- First weight 500g: 115 yuan, additional weight 33 yuan/500g, customs declaration fee 8 yuan
- The weights of 3 parcels are: 700g, 1100g, 1600g

Total shipping fee for separate delivery: 568 yuan
Total shipping fee for centralized delivery: 321 yuan
Savings: 247 yuan (568-321)
Using [Cost Estimation] to calculate shipping costs by transshipment is more intuitive.

## 6. What are the restrictions on sending imitation brands, contraband, liquids, pastes, food, DVD discs, and medicines?

According to conventional transportation experience, the following goods are restricted:

- Cigarettes, alcohol, medicine, and contraband are not accepted for shipment
- Food, liquid, granular, and paste goods need to provide non-dangerous goods appraisal
- A small amount of sensitive goods can be mixed with ordinary goods, for example, cosmetics single bottle ≤100ml, single parcel ≤200ml
