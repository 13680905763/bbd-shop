# Wire Transfer

## Steps

1. Log in to your account, click [Personal Center] – [Recharge].
2. Enter the amount to be recharged and click [Confirm] (the recharge amount needs to be greater than the product amount).
3. Select the wire transfer channel and click [Place Order for Settlement].

> **Processing Time**
>
> Bank processing usually takes a certain amount of time. Under normal circumstances, the recharge amount will be credited to the bbdbuy balance within 3 working days.

## Withdrawal Instructions

If you need to withdraw cash, please find the recharge source channel in [Personal Center], enter the payment password, and the finance department will process it within 1–5 working days. If it has not arrived, please contact online customer service for verification.
