# Insured Parcel / Free Insurance Compensation Standard

The compensation standards for insured parcels purchasing bbdbuy Care Service, as well as free insurance not purchased, are as follows:

**bbdbuy Insurance and Compensation Rules**

1. If the goods are lost after entering the bbdbuy warehouse, and confirmed lost by the staff, the full cost of the goods will be compensated. Parcels without inbound scan records in bbdbuy cannot provide inspection and compensation services;
2. No online information/not picked up and detained by customs cannot be released: If there is no terminal pickup information for 25 days, the shipping fee will be refunded. If there is no terminal pickup information for 30 days, compensation will be 6$/kg.
3. Interrupted update: If the logistics track is not updated for more than 7 working days after the last-mile service provider picks up and transports, an investigation will be opened with the service provider. If there is no result after 7 days of investigation, compensation will be 6$/kg for within 4kg (including 4kg), and 20 Euro/ticket for above 4kg.
   *The following problems and countries are not compensated: unknown/incorrect recipient address, recipient contact information problem, delivery recipient not present, waiting for pickup upon arrival, recipient failure to pick up in time upon arrival at the post office, return on the way and re-dispatch issues. Countries: Spain, Italy, France are not within this interrupted update compensation condition.*
4. Excluding overtime pickup caused by uncontrollable factors such as customs inspection, local workers' strikes, weather reasons, etc.;

**II. Disclaimer**

1. Force majeure factors: including but not limited to: war, natural disasters (including earthquakes, cyclones, storms, floods, etc.), nuclear radiation, nuclear explosion, nuclear pollution and other losses caused by radioactive pollution, resulting in direct or indirect loss or damage;
2. The user cannot provide relevant certificates required for compensation;
3. Exceeding the application compensation time limit (within 7 days of parcel signing or within 45 days of sending without inquiry or compensation request), the parcel is signed for normally during the compensation application period;
4. Theft or disappearance for unknown reasons after delivery;
5. The mailed items contain goods of uninsurable categories, or the mailed items violate the regulations on prohibited or restricted mailing, and are confiscated by the competent authority or handled in accordance with relevant regulations;
6. Loss or loss of mailed items caused by the shipper's responsibility, intentional act or negligence, or the cause of the mailed items themselves;
7. Other circumstances not belonging to compensation liability

*Final compensation is subject to the claims settlement standards of the logistics company and bbdbuy.*
