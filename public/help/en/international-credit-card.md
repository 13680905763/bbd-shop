# International Credit Card Recharge

Log in to your account, click [Personal Center] – [Recharge], and select "International Credit Card" payment method.

## Instructions

1. Enter credit card information: card number, validity period, security code and cardholder information.
2. Confirm the recharge amount and submit payment.

> **Tip**
>
> Recharge arrival time is usually real-time, but special circumstances require 1–5 working days.

If you encounter account problems, please contact customer service for assistance.
