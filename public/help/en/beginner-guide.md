# Beginner Guide: How to Purchase Products via bbdbuy

## Step 1: Choose Products

Method 1: Find the product you want on Taobao/Tmall/1688, copy the product link, and paste it into the search bar.

Method 2: Enter the product name into the bbdbuy search bar and choose from recommended items from Taobao, Tmall, or JD.

Method 3: Use the image search feature to find similar products.

> **Tip**
>
> If you paste a product link and it opens the DIY order page, the product requires manual purchase. Fill in the required details and click “Buy Now” or “Add to Cart”.

## Step 2: Submit Order

Method 1: bbdbuy supports auto-fetching of 99% of product information from Taobao/Tmall/1688. Select color, size, and quantity, then click “Add to Cart” or “Buy Now”.

Method 2: Select products from your cart and click “Checkout” to submit your order.

## Step 3: Pay for the Order

Submit the order, choose your payment method, and pay for the product and domestic shipping fees within China.

> **Tip**
>
> Supports multiple currencies (RMB & foreign currencies). Fill in the destination country and purchase requirements, select optional value-added services, and submit the order.

## Step 4: Purchasing by bbdbuy

The bbdbuy purchasing team will contact the seller and buy the product you ordered.

> **Tip**
>
> Order status flow: Pending Purchase — Ordered — Shipped — Delivered — Arrived — Warehoused. During this period, you can communicate with the agent via “Order Inquiry”.

## Step 5: Inspection and Storage

Once the product arrives at the warehouse, bbdbuy will conduct strict inspection, weighing, and photography. Three free photos will be provided. Enjoy 90 days of free storage and optional extended storage.

> **Tip**
>
> Items stored for more than 180 days (including extensions) will be automatically discarded. You may submit packages or purchase additional items for consolidation at any time.

## Step 6: Submit Parcel

In the 'Warehouse' page, select your items, click Submit Parcel, fill in the international shipping address, and choose a delivery method.

> **Tip**
>
> Some items may have shipping restrictions and only eligible shipping routes will be shown. Optional services include bubble wrap, vacuum bags, stretch film, reinforcement, insurance, etc.

## Step 7: Pay International Shipping Deposit

Choose a payment method and pay the international shipping deposit, which includes customs fees, warehouse handling fees, and optional value-added services.

> **Tip**
>
> You may choose to ship items separately or together. The system will recalculate shipping costs based on weight, volume, and destination. Any difference will be refunded or charged accordingly.

## Step 8: bbdbuy Ships the Parcel

After payment, the warehouse team packs, weighs, and measures the parcel and ships it via reliable international logistics. You can track progress in the Package Details page.

> **Tip**
>
> Parcel status flow: Pending — Processing — Outbound Pending — Outbound — Packed — Weighed — Label Printed — Verified — Handed Over — Shipped — Delivered.

## Step 9: Confirm Receipt & Rewards

After receiving your package, confirm receipt. Your account will earn reward points, which can be redeemed for products or unlock rewards.

> **Tip**
>
> Once you reach 500 points, redeemable products become available. For help, contact customer service.
