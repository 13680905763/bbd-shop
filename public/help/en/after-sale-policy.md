# After-sales Policy Description

To protect user rights and interests, bbdbuy provides a standard after-sales processing process as follows:

**Notes on Receipt of Goods**

1. Before signing for receipt, check whether the outer packaging of the goods is intact, whether the box seal is intact, and whether there is a significant difference in weight.
2. Regardless of whether there is obvious damage to the outer packaging or traces of being torn open, you must inspect the goods in front of the courier before signing for receipt, and make a note on the waybill before signing for receipt, or reject the visa directly if there is a problem.
3. If the parcel not signed for in front of the deliveryman is damaged or missing parts, please keep the outer packaging box (including the sealing tape) and report it to the local post office within 2 days after signing for receipt (subject to the proper delivery time on the logistics provider's website), and provide the goods invoice, physical photos and other information to contact us to apply for compensation from the post office for you.
   *Note: If your parcel is a postal parcel such as EMS, SAL, etc., in addition to the above information (goods invoice, physical photos, etc.), you also need to provide the damage report or case num issued by the post office to us, so as to verify the compensation matters with the logistics provider together.*
4. The normal signing for the goods marks the end of the entire transshipment process. Our company will not be responsible for any situation that occurs to the goods after normal signing for receipt.
5. After the parcel is signed for, if there are any problems, please report to customer service within 72 hours of signing for receipt. Overdue requests will not be accepted.

**Disclaimer**

1. Fragile and vulnerable items, whether or not enhanced packaging is selected, only participate in loss claims and do not participate in damage claims.
2. Dangerous goods and contraband do not participate in claims (e.g., compressed gas, liquefied gas, flammable liquid, acid-base corrosive goods and other national embargoed goods).
3. Losses or expenses caused by natural loss, essential defects, characteristics of goods, and rise and fall of market prices do not participate in claims.
4. Total or partial loss caused by natural disasters such as bad weather, lightning, tsunami, earthquake, flood during transportation.
5. Goods that exceed the shelf life, warranty period, or delivery cycle due to force majeure factors such as weather and customs clearance efficiency do not participate in compensation.
6. Losses, responsibilities and consequences caused by customs reasons requiring your cooperation and assistance in relevant operations (including but not limited to paying customs duties), but your refusal to cooperate prevents the parcel from being delivered within the time limit promised by the transshipment service provider, shall be borne by you, and the transshipment logistics provider shall not bear any responsibility.
